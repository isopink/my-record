Overview

A Claude skill that researches a topic along several axes, rechecks every cited number and writes a Korean report. What comes out is one A4 PDF in the bundled Editorial Mono design.

It runs six steps and never skips one: ask, research, verify, write, self-check and render to PDF. It only asks for what is missing, such as what the report should decide, what it covers and who will read it. Research runs in parallel, one agent per topic plus two more axes, segments and existing alternatives with their prices. One agent per two notes then opens every cited URL and marks each number confirmed, mismatched or unconfirmed in a separate ledger, and the report takes its numbers only from that ledger. A single writer drafts 15,000 to 25,000 Korean characters, the Markdown becomes HTML and then an A4 PDF, and the key pages are checked as images.

Method
parallel research agents, a verification round, one writer, PDF render
Install
zip upload to claude.ai Skills

Motivation

The reports I write change topic every time, but the research behind them looks much the same at the top level. I wanted to know whether that part could be automated.
