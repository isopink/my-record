Job Interview Prep

Rules pick the findings, Solar Pro 4 writes, a verifier checks.

Built with
Upstage Solar Pro 4, OpenDART API, Python 3.12 with FastAPI, Render

Overview

A web service that reads a listed company's DART business report for a chosen role. It shows notable findings with numbers and sources, then asks 3 to 7 interview questions ending on "why this company?"

Data
OpenDART filings and optional IR PDFs (one file, 20 MB or less)
Method
rule judge, 6 finding types by 6 role families, Solar Pro 4 writer, figure verifier
Ran on
Render free tier

Problem define

Applicants open the business report and close it, not knowing where to look. One quoted applicant called it "studying for an exam with no syllabus."

  • About 7 hours to prepare the reasons for one company
  • 28.6% of 1,226 job seekers named the motivation question hardest

Solution

A service that turns one company's filings into findings, sources and role-specific questions. It never writes the answer; the applicant picks and writes, and answers stay in the browser.

Value with unitWhat it measures (10 words or fewer)
97.3% of 2,666 figuresReport figures matching the DART source, 200 listed companies
21 sMedian time per report, 3 requests at a time

Process

  1. Skill. The preliminary round engine was a Claude skill: judge.py picks findings by rule, verify.py rechecks figures.
  2. Service. The same scripts were wrapped in a FastAPI server, with Solar Pro 4 writing only inside the 42-cell mapping table.
  3. Patents. A KIPRIS patent section was added on 09-15 and removed the same day; uploaded IR PDF analysis replaced it.
  4. Verify. Amounts are checked within 100 million won, ratios within 0.5 points; after two failed format retries the report falls back to rule findings.
