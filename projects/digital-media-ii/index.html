Overview

Coursework for Digital Media II, a course on carrying a parametric design through digital tools to a finished result. The final piece is "Seoul Legacy: The Architect's Gambit", a team game concept with a physical game-board model.

Course
Digital Media II, UOSARCH, fall 2024
Final result
a physical model shaped like a handheld console, with one district of Seoul in coloured blocks
Midterm
"A Tension Is all you Need", a 70-floor parametric tower, individual

Problem define

The final brief asked how Seoul's traffic, density and lack of green space could be shown as play, using real city data. The levels had to come from data, not be drawn by hand.

  • 7.9542 trillion won: Seoul traffic congestion cost, about 44% of the national urban total, as quoted on 디미 죄종발표자료/슬라이드4.JPG (outside source, not measured by the team)
  • 426: stages (dong) in the game structure (디미 죄종발표자료/슬라이드22.JPG, "스테이지 (426개)")

Solution

Each dong gets a level from 1 to 10 per category by comparing counts, locations and density of public data, weighted by the age mix of residents. The model shows each category as a colour and each level as block height.

Value with unitWhat it measures (10 words or fewer)
1 to 10Level range per dong per category
6Quality-of-life categories used as parameters
11Shapefiles read into the Grasshopper calculator

The six categories: culture and leisure, public wifi, traffic, air quality, safety, green space. Age weights (1 to 3) are set for three groups, 0 to 19, 20 to 59 and 60 and over; safety is not weighted.
