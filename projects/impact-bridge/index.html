Overview

Impact Bridge is a student project that links social enterprises short on marketing staff with university marketing societies for month-long projects. The firm gets marketing work done; the students get real practice, feedback and a portfolio piece.

Shown at
Enactus Korea new project registration, 2026
Data
survey of 12 social enterprises, plus interviews and desk research
Method
brokered short projects between a firm and a marketing society
Team
3 students, University of Seoul

Problem define

Social enterprises run on a few staff, so the representative carries most of the work and marketing drops. Marketing students, in turn, lack chances to do real work.

  • 12 of 12 surveyed social enterprises said they need marketing staff.
  • 100 of 5,040 certified social enterprises (2.0%) get the public portal's custom marketing support.

Solution

Built so far: a brokerage model with a mission statement, key activities and a fee-based revenue line, registered with Enactus Korea.

Process

  1. Research. Desk research on social enterprise finance and support, for example 46% of 3,560 firms posting an operating loss.
  2. Survey. Surveyed 12 social enterprises and ran interviews; all asked for marketing staff that is affordable, responsible and understands social value.
  3. Model. Chose month-long projects with marketing societies that already run industry partnerships, over two-week job matching like Mini Intern.
  4. Register. Filed the new project with Enactus Korea; the cost box of the business model was left blank.

Lesson

So far it is a plan on paper: demand checked, delivery not yet tested.

The cost side of the model is blank, and the only revenue line is a brokerage fee with no price set. The survey asked for low cost, but no price was put to them. The next proof is one finished match with a firm's own verdict.
