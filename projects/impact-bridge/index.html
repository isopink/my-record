Overview

Impact Bridge is a student project that links social enterprises short on marketing staff with university marketing societies for month-long projects. The firm gets marketing work done; the students get real practice, feedback and a portfolio piece.

We started with desk research on how social enterprises are funded and supported; 46% of 3,560 firms, for example, reported an operating loss. We then surveyed 12 social enterprises and interviewed them, and every one asked for marketing staff who are affordable, responsible and understand social value. That led us to month-long projects with marketing societies that already run industry partnerships, rather than two-week job matching like Mini Intern. So far we have built a brokerage model with a mission statement, key activities and a fee-based revenue line, and registered it with Enactus Korea. Next, we plan to pivot toward HR.

Data
survey of 12 social enterprises, plus interviews and desk research
Method
brokered short projects between a firm and a marketing society

Motivation

Students say there are no jobs, and companies say there are no people. Is that really true?
